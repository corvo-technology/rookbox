## How much does it cost?

Usage is measured (running machines, memory, disk), but billing is not enabled in the beta. You follow your usage on the **Usage** page of the [Dashboard](https://app.rookbox.run). Billing in Brazilian reais with Pix is planned, with no price or date set.

## Is there support in Portuguese?

Yes. Support is in Portuguese.

## Do I need to change my E2B code?

No. Set `E2B_API_KEY` and `E2B_API_URL` and your code stays the same. What differs and what does not exist is in [Compatibility](/en/reference/compat/).

## Can I choose the machine size?

Through the SDK, no: it is always 2 vCPU, 2 GiB RAM and 10 GiB disk.

## My machine disappeared. Why?

Its timeout ended. Without auto-suspend, the end of the timeout removes the machine. See [Lifecycle](/en/concepts/lifecycle/).

## Does the machine have internet?

Yes, open. See [Security](/en/security/).

## Where is my data?

On the machine's disk, which is yours until you remove it. Suspending keeps disk and memory. Removing deletes everything.

## I got a 429. Now what?

It can be the 3-machine limit or the request limit. The message says which. See [Errors](/en/reference/errors/).
